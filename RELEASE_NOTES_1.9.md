# OpenCody 1.9 – App Store release notes

Optimized for iPhone Duo. On the outer display OpenCody looks and works like it always has on iPhone. Unfold it and you get the sidebar layout from iPad: your projects on the left, the chat on the right. The toolbar and tab bar move to the side where the system puts them, and never cover the message field or a diff.

Folding and unfolding keeps your place. The server, the project, the open chat, how far you had scrolled and the message you were typing all stay where they were.

Fixes:
• On iPad, resizing OpenCody in Split View no longer jumps back to the project list.
• "No Server Connected" in the sidebar is no longer cut off in narrow windows.
• Files, Todos and Diff open beside the chat when there is room, and as a sheet when there isn't.
• Notifications for the chat you have open stay quiet, as they were always meant to.
